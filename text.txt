Take one slow breath.

Leave the noise of the day outside for a moment.

What comes next was made quietly...
for someone who notices everything.




There’s a girl who can read my mind,  
In ways I never planned or designed.  
I’ll say something careless, half-joke, half-air,  
And somehow she’s already there.

I ask her questions I already know,  
Just to watch her smile a little slow.  
Then when she answers, calm and kind,  
I laugh—“Wow… you read my mind.”

In lines of code where I lose my way,  
And bugs decide they’d like to stay,  
I leave behind what I could not see,  
She finds it all so easily.

A hidden flaw, a missing piece,  
She notices both without a crease.  
Like every thought I meant to hide,  
She somehow sees the truth inside.

She’s logic wrapped in something warm,  
A steady calm through every storm.  
Where I am hurried, loud, and blind,  
She is clarity of mind.

But somewhere between the jokes I make,  
And all the “mistakes” I sometimes fake,  
There’s something real I cannot hide,  
No clever turn to set aside.

Because it isn’t only code or play,  
Or random things we loosely say.  
It’s how she knows without a sign,  
The quiet thoughts I still call mine.

There’s a girl who can read my mind,  
Not just the loud, but the undefined.  
And maybe one day I’ll be brave enough to say—  
I think you’ve known all along anyway.

And maybe one day I’ll be brave enough to say—  
Though I think you already know.  
Then again…  
You always read my mind.
